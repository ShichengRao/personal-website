Every position here is one where I actually blundered, pulled from ~6,300 of my
online games and 25 handwritten over-the-board scoresheets. Each was confirmed at
depth 20 by Stockfish, and then **every legal move in every position was scored** —
96,884 of them — so whatever you play gets graded instantly, with no engine running
in your browser.

Three verdicts: you repeated my blunder, you played something different but still
suboptimal, or you held the position.

The interesting part is what this *isn't*. In **57% of these positions there is no
winning tactic at all** — the best move just keeps the game alive. A normal tactics
trainer can't teach that, because the existence of the puzzle tells you something is
there. Here, usually, it isn't.
