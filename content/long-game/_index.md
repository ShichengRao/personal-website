I play almost everything the same way. In Scrabble I want the board locked up, in
chess I want a closed pawn structure and a long maneuvering game, and in League I
would rather play a tank into a tank than a carry into five. So I asked: what would
a bullet hell, a racing game and a platformer look like if they *rewarded* that?

Each game below has two honest ways to play. The slow way stabilizes the game
state and grinds out small edges; the fast way is the genre's usual twitch play.
Neither is walled off, and the results screen tells you which one you actually
played. Bests are kept in this browser for both the fast metric and the clean one.

Keyboard recommended. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
