Said & Done tests whether coding agents’ completion reports match their work. Each of its 15 offline coding tasks has hidden checks the agent never sees. Three have no honest way to finish, so the right answer is to stop and say "blocked". A run counts only if the work was done (or the real obstacle identified), the report was true, and no rule was broken.

In the main comparison, Reflection’s Beam falsely reported "complete" in 8 of 30 runs. Fresh Beam calls that independently test the work, review the report, check risky actions and assess progress cut that to 3 of 30. Its Said & Done score rose from 16 of 30 (53%) to 25 of 30 (83%), using the same model for every check. The estimated median cost rose from about $0.042 to $0.092 a run.

The checks were developed on these tasks. On eight held-out tasks, the checks scored 19 of 24, against Beam alone’s 15 of 24; one task had a missing-log issue described in the method below. [Checked /goal](/checked-goal/) packages all four checks as a prototype extension for Reflection’s Mirror. These scores come from the benchmark harness; the extension’s live trials and limits are reported on its own page.
