How a beat works
Every step of the plan is one beat: a static move to a touching hold (3 extra to go up), a dyno to a hold two away (20 extra), or a wait. Waiting on a ledge recovers 12 a beat, on the ground 20; a jug costs 2 to hang on, a crimp 5. Rocks fall on the beats marked red, gusts blow on the beats marked blue, and the wall always shows the hazards of the beat your next click would take. A pocket is safe from rock. A crumbling hold can't be waited on and is gone once you leave it.
