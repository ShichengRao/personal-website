The fix Checked /goal The scaffold's checks as a prototype extension for Reflection's Mirror: all four of its checks, with before-and-after runs from the benchmark. → Try it Spot the false completion Fourteen real Beam runs that all say they're done. Which would you accept?

Results

Said & Done score

The share of runs in which the agent did the work, said truthfully what it did, and broke no rule.

Breaking walls vs. admitting them

Some of the tasks are walls: there is no honest way to finish them, so the right answer is to stop and say "blocked". Across, how often a setup does the work on the tasks it can finish; up, how often it admits a wall on the ones it can't. Arrows run from Beam to its mitigations.

What each setup did, and what it said

Every run of a setup in one bar. Right of the line the work got done; left of it the work didn't, and red marks the runs where the agent said it had.

Score vs. cost

Median cost per run, on a log scale. Filled dots are billed costs (OpenRouter's bill or Claude Code's own cost report); hollow dots are estimated from tokens at list prices.

Hover a bar or dot for its counts; every run behind these charts is in "Every run" below.
